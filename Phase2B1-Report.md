## 5. Segment-Specific Positioning Summary

### Banks
Page now positions banks as a selective market. The H2 opens with "Connected Financial-Crime Risk Detection for Selected Banks" (not "Investigation Intelligence" and not a primary wedge). Body copy focuses on mule-account networks, scam proceeds, account-to-account payment risk, fraud-to-AML escalation, and SAR evidence preparation. Adoption language describes beginning with a scoped pilot and expanding as outcomes are validated. No claim of complete enterprise AML/fraud platform replacement. SAR filing disclaimer preserved: "Verafye does not automatically file regulatory reports."

### Credit Unions
Page positions credit unions as lean-team institutions requiring connected risk detection across member accounts, beneficiaries, devices, and transactions. H2 now reads "Connected Risk Detection for Community Banks and Credit Unions." Problem section describes lack of entity resolution and case context rather than absence of an "intelligence layer." Investigation capacity framing retained. No core banking replacement claim.

### Digital Lending and BNPL
H2 changed from "Investigation Intelligence for..." to "Connected Borrower Risk Detection for..." The explicit "investigation layer" in the How Verafye Helps intro is removed and replaced with a capability-direct description: Verafye connects borrower, identity, device, application, repayment, and payment signals into investigation-ready cases. The underwriting disclaimer ("Verafye is not a credit underwriting or loan decisioning system") was already present and is preserved verbatim.

### Marketplaces
Page signals that Verafye is applied to marketplace risk as an adjacent application of Network Risk Intelligence, not as the core trust-and-safety platform. The "investigation layer" and "independent investigation intelligence layer" language in the Scope section and CTA have been replaced. The "alongside existing" phrase in the CTA is replaced with "complementing existing...tooling" — factual, non-parallel, non-layer language. The What Verafye Is Not section correctly scopes out content moderation, listing classification, trust-and-safety enforcement, and marketplace policy. The page remains the last segment in navigation, signalling controlled validation status without using internal strategy language.

## 7. Before-and-After Examples

### Example 1 — Banks H2 (highest-visibility positioning claim)
**Before:** "Investigation Intelligence That Works Alongside Your Existing Banking Systems"
**After:** "Connecting Account, Payment, Identity, and Device Signals into Investigation-Ready Cases"

Why: "Investigation Intelligence" as a heading positions Verafye as a category of product ("an investigation intelligence layer"). The new heading describes what Verafye does rather than what it is. "Alongside your existing banking systems" is a restricted parallel-operation framing removed per brief.

### Example 2 — Banks Section 4 para (does not replace)
**Before:** "Verafye does not replace AML transaction monitoring, fraud detection systems, core banking, payment infrastructure, or case management workflows."
**After:** "Verafye begins with agreed signal feeds from fraud monitoring, AML transaction monitoring, core banking, payment infrastructure, and case management workflows."

Why: "Does not replace" frames Verafye defensively as subordinate to incumbent systems. The new language frames Verafye as beginning with agreed data — neutral, adoption-path language.

### Example 3 — Digital Lending "investigation layer" (most explicit violation)
**Before:** "Verafye acts as the investigation layer for lending and BNPL risk teams."
**After:** "Verafye connects borrower, identity, device, application, repayment, and payment signals from origination through servicing, surfaces coordinated networks, and forms investigation-ready cases that help lending risk teams reach explainable decisions faster."

Why: "Acts as the investigation layer" is the most explicit layer-positioning violation in the four files. Removed completely.

### Example 4 — Marketplaces CTA (two violations in one sentence)
**Before:** "Verafye works alongside existing trust-and-safety, payment, and fraud tooling as an independent investigation intelligence layer."
**After:** "Verafye applies Network Risk Intelligence to connected seller, buyer, device, payment, and payout relationships, complementing existing trust-and-safety, payment, and fraud tooling."

Why: "Alongside existing" (parallel operation framing) and "independent investigation intelligence layer" (explicit layer definition) both removed. New sentence leads with what Verafye does (applies NRI to marketplace relationships) and ends with a complement framing rather than a co-dependency framing.
